# Start chatting on Microsoft Teams

WednesdayAI appears in Microsoft Teams as a bot that your administrator or IT department has installed for your organisation. You can chat with it privately or mention it in team channels.

## What you need

- Microsoft Teams from your organisation
- The bot installed by your administrator or IT team (ask them if you cannot find it)

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the bot">
    In Teams, tap the search bar and type the bot's name (your administrator can tell you what it is called). Bots you can chat with directly appear under **People** or **Bots** in the results. Your administrator may also have pinned it to a team or channel.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send a private message">
    Select the bot from the search results and type your message in the chat. The bot will reply within a few seconds.

    <Note>
      The first time you message the bot you may receive a **pairing code** — a short reply with an 8-character code — instead of a normal answer. Send that code to your administrator. Once they approve it, message the bot again and it will answer normally. Codes expire after one hour; if that happens, just message the bot again for a fresh one.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use it in channels and group chats">
    In a team channel or group chat, mention the bot to get its attention:

    > @WednesdayAI can you summarise this thread?

    In channels, the bot's reply usually appears in a thread under your message, keeping the main channel tidy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips

- **Mention to be noticed** — in channels and group chats the bot only responds when you mention it with `@`. In a private chat, every message gets a reply.
- **Everyone in the channel sees the conversation** — ask personal or sensitive questions in a private chat instead.
- **Files** — you can send and receive files in a private chat with the bot. Support for files in channels and group chats depends on how your administrator set it up.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The bot does not respond in a channel or group chat">
    Mention the bot by typing `@` and selecting it from the suggestions. If mentioning does not work, that channel or group chat may not be enabled for the bot — ask your administrator. Group chats are switched off by default.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot find the bot in search">
    The bot app may not be installed for you yet. Ask your administrator or IT department to make it available in your Teams organisation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My private messages are ignored">
    You may be waiting for pairing approval — send the 8-character code you received to your administrator, then message the bot again. If you never received a code, ask your administrator to check the bot's access settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The bot says I am not allowed">
    Your Teams account may not be on the bot's allowed list yet. Ask your administrator to add you.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
