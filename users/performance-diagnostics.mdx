# Performance diagnostics

Performance diagnostics help the person who runs your WednesdayAI find out why replies are slow, why a conversation got stuck, or why the service is using too much memory or computer time. You do not need to change anything on your side — diagnostics run in the background and do not change how you chat.

When diagnostics are switched on, WednesdayAI records timing, error, and resource signals about the running service. These signals help answer questions like "was WednesdayAI busy at that moment", "was a tool slow", "was the AI provider slow", and "did memory grow during that time".

## What you may notice

Most diagnostics run quietly. Your chat experience should look exactly the same.

During a focused investigation, the person who runs WednesdayAI may temporarily collect deeper information from the running service. You may notice slower replies during that short window, especially while a memory snapshot or a performance profile is being taken. This is temporary and ends when the investigation ends.

## What diagnostics do not mean

Diagnostics are not a transcript review feature. They explain service health and speed — not what was said.

The people who run WednesdayAI are advised not to collect your raw messages, private tokens, phone numbers, or chat identifiers unless there is a specific reason and a safe place to store them. Routine dashboards use general labels like model names and channel names instead of conversation content.

## Report a slow or stuck reply

If WednesdayAI is slow or a reply never arrives, send the operator enough detail to find the matching moment in their records.

<Steps>
<Step title="Note when it happened">
Write down the approximate local time when the slow reply started, and which chat app you were using (for example Telegram, Discord, WhatsApp, iMessage, or the web chat).
</Step>
<Step title="Note what happened">
Did the reply eventually arrive, arrive with an error message, or never arrive at all? Say which one.
</Step>
<Step title="Note what you were doing">
In general terms, what were you trying to do? Also mention whether this happened once or keeps happening.
</Step>
<Step title="Send it to the operator">
Send those details to the person who runs WednesdayAI for you. You should see a normal reply from them confirming they received it.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
The time, the chat app, and "arrived / errored / never arrived" are the three details that matter most. With those, the operator can usually find the exact moment in their records without needing anything private from you.
</Tip>

<Warning>
Do not send passwords, private tokens, or screenshots that show credentials. Do not paste full conversation text unless the operator asks for it and gives you a safe place to share it.
</Warning>

## During an investigation

The operator may ask you to retry the same action while diagnostics are switched on. After you retry, tell them:

- The time you retried.
- Whether the reply was faster, slower, or unchanged.
- Whether WednesdayAI gave an error message.

This lets the operator compare the before and after signals without needing your private conversation content.

## If it keeps happening

If slow or missing replies repeat after the operator has looked into it, follow the general steps in [Troubleshooting](/users/troubleshooting) and let the operator know the problem returned — include the new time window so they can compare it against the earlier one.
